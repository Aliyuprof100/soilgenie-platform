# 🌱 SoilGenie

### AI-Powered Soil Intelligence for Smallholder Farmers

SoilGenie is an AgTech platform designed to help farmers make better decisions by transforming soil test data into **simple, actionable, and farmer-friendly recommendations**.

Our vision is simple:

> **Test the soil. Understand the soil. Improve the farm.**

---

## 🌍 The Problem

Millions of smallholder farmers make important decisions about:

- 🌱 What crops to plant
- 🧪 Which nutrients their soil needs
- 💧 How to manage irrigation
- 🌾 How to improve soil fertility
- 💰 How to increase productivity

without having access to affordable, timely, and understandable soil intelligence.

Traditional soil testing can also produce technical laboratory results that are difficult for farmers to interpret and translate into practical farm decisions.

SoilGenie is being built to bridge this gap.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

SoilGenie combines:

**Soil Testing + Digital Data + Soil Intelligence + Recommendations**

to help transform raw soil measurements into information farmers can understand and use.

```text
